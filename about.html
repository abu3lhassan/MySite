About

Technology leadership, shaped by operational experience.

I connect business requirements, technology priorities, and practical execution, with a technical foundation built in day-to-day IT operations.

01

My story

I began my IT career in December 2017, progressing through support, mobility, systems, and infrastructure roles. In 2021 I established the IT department at Gas Station Development Company (Orange), and I have led, managed, and developed the IT function ever since: first as System and Network Engineer and, since January 2022, as IT Manager and Head of Technology. My responsibilities span technology planning, service delivery, infrastructure and cloud, cybersecurity, enterprise applications and ERP, and the vendors and projects supporting them. Experience in holding, industrial, service, and fuel retail environments shapes how I assess both business needs and operational constraints.

02

How I lead

I start by understanding the requirement and its operational priority. I work with management, departments, technical colleagues, and vendors to evaluate options, clarify responsibilities, and sequence delivery. My technical background helps me question feasibility and support requirements, while my management role keeps decisions connected to business needs, testing, user adoption, and ongoing service.

03

Independent technical practice

Outside my employment role, I develop and direct selected independent initiatives such as Ali OS and ShareAdd. They provide a practical setting for requirements, information design, interfaces, testing, and iterative improvement. They support my technical understanding alongside my responsibility for enterprise IT.
