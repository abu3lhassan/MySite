About

A technology leader who connects business strategy with reliable execution.

I lead technology as a business function: setting direction, running dependable operations, and making sure every technology investment serves a clear organizational purpose.

01

My story

My career has moved from hands-on support and infrastructure into leading the full technology function. Today, as IT Manager and Head of Technology at Gas Station Development Company (Orange), I am responsible for IT strategy, operations and service delivery, infrastructure and cloud, cybersecurity, enterprise applications and ERP, and the vendors and projects behind them. That technical foundation still shapes how I lead: I know what it takes to keep systems running, so I plan for reliability, security, and scale from the start.

02

How I lead

I start from business priorities, not tools. I work with leadership and department heads to agree on what technology must achieve, then put governance around it: clear ownership, sound policies, controlled access, and accountable vendors and projects. Technology leadership is cross-functional by nature, and the real test is whether the business can rely on it.

03

Beyond work

Books and simple living are part of how I think. They keep me curious, grounded, and aware that complexity is not always intelligence. Sometimes the strongest system is the one that removes noise.
