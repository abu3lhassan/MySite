Professional leadership

Initiatives I lead and coordinate in my current role.

  1. 01Strategic program

    Operations Automation & Integration

    Ongoing · phased

    Multi-site operations depend on dependable, connected systems. I lead the technology side of a phased automation and integration program, translating operational priorities into requirements and coordinating the business, technology partners, and site teams involved.

    Each phase moves through planning, technical evaluation, vendor coordination, and integration into daily operations before the next begins. The aim is clearer operational visibility and less manual effort, introduced in a controlled, supportable way.

    My role: planning, vendor coordination, technical evaluation, delivery oversight, and operational integration.

  2. 02Enterprise systems · ERP

    Enterprise Business Applications

    Ongoing responsibility

    The ERP environment supports core business processes, so it has to stay controlled, available, and aligned with how the organization works. I am responsible for administering and improving it on Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, together with related enterprise applications.

    That covers system administration and permissions, operational support, coordinating business-process changes with departments, prioritizing improvements, and managing the vendor relationship, so that changes are requested, assessed, and delivered in a controlled way.

    My role: system administration, permissions, operational support, process coordination, improvements, and vendor management.

  3. 03Governance · Organizational design

    Organizational Governance Initiatives

    Approved · implementation underway

    Organizations need clear answers to who decides, who is responsible, and how authority is delegated. I led and coordinated the development of governance frameworks covering organizational structures, responsibility models, delegation of authority, and policy documentation.

    Working with the relevant stakeholders, the frameworks were documented, reviewed, and approved, and implementation is now underway. They give the organization an agreed reference for responsibilities and decision-making as adoption progresses.

    My role: structured information design, governance documentation, and stakeholder coordination.

  4. 04Process digitization

    Digital Workflow Solutions

    Manual requests and approvals slow work down and make follow-up hard to track. I define requirements and design digital request and approval workflows, then coordinate development, testing, and handover to users.

    The aim is clear process steps, defined responsibilities, and practical adoption, so that digital workflows replace manual follow-up rather than adding another layer.

    My role: process analysis and functional design, with implementation coordinated across the people involved.
